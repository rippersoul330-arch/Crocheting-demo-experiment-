Our story

A studio built around patience

Loop & Ivy began at a kitchen table with one hook, a basket of leftover wool, and the belief that the things we live with should be made slowly and kept forever.

Today, a tiny collective of makers crochets every order by hand from responsibly farmed wool, organic cotton and plant-dyed alpaca. No factories, no waste, no rush — just the honest texture of handwork.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Everything about ordering, care and shipping. Still curious? Just ask.

How long until my order ships?

Every piece is crocheted by hand once you order, so ready-to-ship items post within 2–3 weeks and custom commissions within 3–5 weeks. You'll get a note the moment yours is on its way.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes! I love bespoke work. Use the Custom Orders form above to share your idea and I'll reply within two business days with a quote and a little mock-up.

How do I care for my crochet piece?

Most pieces love a gentle cool hand-wash and flat drying to keep their shape. Each order arrives with a care card specific to its fibre, and baby items are made to be machine-washable.

What yarns and fibres do you use?

Only natural and responsibly sourced fibres — organic cotton, ethically farmed merino and lambswool, and plant-dyed alpaca. No acrylics, and packaging is always plastic-free.

What is your returns policy?

Ready-made pieces can be returned within 30 days in original condition for a full refund. Because custom commissions are made just for you, they're non-refundable — but I'll always work with you until you're delighted.

Where do you ship?

We ship anywhere in India with tracked delivery, and shipping is free on orders over ₹1,500. Prefer to collect in person? Choose local pickup at checkout. Delivery is typically 3–7 days after your piece is ready.
